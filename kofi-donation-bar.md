### Ko-fi Donation Bar (gaston1799)
## Description:
```
Injects a live donation progress bar under the Support section on ko-fi.com/gaston1799. Powered by the public ko-fi-donations feed: shows goal progress ($ raised of $370), the donor list, and an empty state until the first donation arrives.
```

```
1.0.0
```
